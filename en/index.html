    A new chapter

    The INDEX club's oldest project, relaunched

    The fund is the oldest project of the INDEX Capital Markets Student Club. For more than 25 years it has passed on knowledge of capital markets to each new intake. Since autumn 2026 it has run in a new format: a portfolio valued daily against the S&P 500, analyses published under their authors' names and its own website showing every decision.

      Index Fund · INDEX Capital Markets Student Club, Krakow University of Economics

      Index Fund is an educational student project run by Fundacja Index (KRS 0001137760). It is not an investment fund within the meaning of the Polish Act of 27 May 2004 on investment funds, it does not raise money for investment and it does not provide brokerage services. The portfolio is virtual. The analyses express the views of their authors and are not investment advice.
