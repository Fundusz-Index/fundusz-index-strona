About

Index Fund is the oldest project of the INDEX Capital Markets Student Club at Krakow University of Economics. For more than 25 years we have passed on knowledge of capital markets to each new intake. We operate within Fundacja Koła Naukowego Index, the club's foundation.

We teach what lectures do not: where to find information, how to verify it and filter out the noise, how to value a company and defend a thesis. Members work on the tasks analysts and investors face every day. Since autumn 2026 every portfolio decision has come with an analysis, authors and a date.

Why the fund exists

A student fund modelled on those at US universities

At many American universities students manage a fund themselves, often with the university's real money. They value companies, defend their ideas in front of the team and answer for the result. In Poland and across Central and Eastern Europe such projects are still rare. Index Fund brings this model to Krakow University of Economics.

Our portfolio is virtual. The analyses, the vote on every company and the daily valuation work just as they do there.

  • Learning on the real market

    We value companies using data that changes every day and work on the tasks professional analysts face. Discounted cash flow is the basis of our valuations.

  • Open decisions

    Every decision comes with an analysis, authors and a date. We show the result against the S&P 500, including when it is worse.

  • Contact with practitioners

    Workshops, events and conferences with people who work in capital markets. We learn from them and show them our work.

  • Passing knowledge on

    We have been collecting knowledge for more than 25 years: over 300 investment pitches and 60 studies. It passes to each new intake instead of leaving with the outgoing board.

How we make decisions

  1. Step 1AnalysisA team values the company: DCF and comparable valuation.
  2. Step 2PitchA pitch to the fund's members, followed by questions.
  3. Step 3DecisionFund members vote after the pitch. A company enters the portfolio when a majority backs it.
  4. Step 4PublicationThe trade goes into the portfolio and the analysis onto the website.
